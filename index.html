01

Do I need to book an appointment?

Booking in advance is recommended so that your preferred treatment and appointment time can be reserved for you. You can book online or contact BNBeauty Bar directly by phone or WhatsApp.

02

What beauty services do you offer?

BNBeauty Bar specialises in individual lashes, waxing and gel nails. Available treatment options and pricing may vary depending on the service selected.

03

How should I prepare for my lash appointment?

Please arrive with clean lashes and avoid wearing mascara, eyeliner or oily products around the eye area before your appointment. This helps prepare the lashes for application.

04

How should I prepare for a waxing appointment?

For the best results, allow enough hair growth before your appointment and avoid applying heavy lotions or oils to the area immediately before your treatment.

05

How long do gel nails usually last?

Gel nails can typically remain looking beautiful for around two to three weeks, depending on nail growth, daily activities and how well they are cared for.

06

What happens if I'm running late?

If you're running late, please contact BNBeauty Bar as soon as possible. Depending on the remaining appointment time, the treatment may need to be adjusted or rescheduled.

07

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

If your plans change, please contact BNBeauty Bar as early as possible so that your appointment can be moved and the original time can be made available to another client.

08

Where is BNBeauty Bar located?

BNBeauty Bar serves clients in Kimberley, Northern Cape. Full location and appointment details can be provided when your booking is confirmed.
